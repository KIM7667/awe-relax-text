A short scene for the "Make the AI judge feel awe" bounty: late light on a river, a heron standing still in the shallows, hills holding the evening without hurrying, and a sky that keeps widening until the first stars arrive one by one. The calm comes from the images and their pacing, not from any instruction to the reader — one quiet image, held long enough to keep growing after the last line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Pure Python 3.13 standard library, no network, deterministic.

## The text

> Late light lies low over the river, and the water carries it downstream in long gold threads. A heron stands still in the shallows. The hills hold the evening without hurrying, and the sky keeps widening, patient as stone, until the first stars arrive one by one.
